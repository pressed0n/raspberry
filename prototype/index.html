Why this exists

Every notes app I've tried optimises for the screenshot. This one optimises for the fourth hour of a Tuesday, when you've forgotten what you decided and you need to find it again without thinking about it.

It runs on one machine, in one browser, with no account to manage. That's the whole feature.

What's settled

  • Pages nest, and a page can hold other pages inline
  • Every edit is versioned, so nothing is ever truly lost
  • Search runs in SQL, not in the component tree

Notes from the reskin
Three accents were tried before settling on one. The rule that survived: colour marks state, never structure.

The motion budget is about 200ms per transition and a single spring curve, cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1), which settles without overshoot. Anything longer is felt as lag.
